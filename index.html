Tips and tricks

  • Two thumbs. Hold a chord with the left thumb, drag the strings with the right. Slow drag sounds like a harp, a quick flick like a guitar strum.
  • No theory needed. Press Mood, pick a feeling and a flavour, press Use. Every chord on screen now fits together.
  • Home chord. The button marked HOME is where the music rests. Start on it and finish on it, and anything in between sounds like a song.
  • Instant song. In Mood finder press a Load button. The chords go into a row. Press Play, then Next on each bar.
  • Hands free. Song, then Every bar. The chords change by themselves, so you can strum or play lead on top.
  • Bend. Hold a chord and slide your finger onto a neighbour. The pitch glides with your finger. Slide back to cancel.
  • Lead. Switch the plate to Lead. Every note belongs to the scale. Bright strings belong to the current chord, so land on those.
  • Lead feel. Glide makes notes slide into each other. Vibrato adds wobble on held notes. Echo fills the gaps.
  • Arp. Up-Down at 1/16 with 2 octaves gives a classic synth run. Chord pattern at 1/8 gives a piano pulse.
  • Chord colours. 7th is smoother and jazzier. sus4 feels open and unfinished. add9 is dreamy.
  • Hold. With Hold on, the chord keeps ringing. Tap the same chord again to silence it.
  • Tempo. Tap the number in time with the speed you want.
  • Sound delay. Tap a chord once, then reopen Tips to see this phone's sound delay. Bluetooth speakers and earbuds add a lot of delay. Use the phone speaker or wired earphones.
  • No sound? Check the phone's media volume and silent mode, then tap any chord once.
